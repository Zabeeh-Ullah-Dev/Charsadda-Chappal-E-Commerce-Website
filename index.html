The Ancestral Workshop

Molded by History.
Stitched by Hand.

The Charsadda chappal is not mere footwear; it is an enduring symbol of Pashtun identity, resilience, and masculine grace. Originating in the fertile Peshawar valley, this distinctive sandal was engineered to conquer rugged mountain terrain with unshakeable fortitude.

Every sole is sliced and shaped from heavy-duty rubber treads, bonded to layers of vegetable-tanned leather with thick nylon cord. No fast-fashion shortcuts. Just honest, timeless leatherwork honed over a century.

The Peshawar Valley

Where Every Stitch
Tells a Dynasty.

Charsadda, situated at the confluence of the Swat and Kabul rivers, was once the ancient Gandharan capital of Pushkalavati. For hundreds of years, leather workers here have forged a style so distinct that wearing a genuine Charsadda chappal is an immediate statement of poise and respect throughout South Asia.

At KHURO, we honor this unbroken chain of mastery. We partner directly with artisan workshops in Charsadda to deliver authentic craft straight to your doorstep without compromising quality or tradition.
